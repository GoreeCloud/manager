# GoreeCloud Manager — Competitive Objectives

GoreeCloud Manager should become a first-party administration console for GoreeCloud rather than a thin rebrand of a third-party dashboard.

## Objectives

Manager should match or exceed mature infrastructure and platform consoles in:

- authenticated operational visibility;
- dependency and service health presentation;
- privacy- and security-aware administration;
- recovery and readiness visibility;
- least-privilege integration design;
- failure and stale-evidence communication;
- responsive and accessible administration;
- deployment, rollback, and provenance clarity.

Manager should intentionally differ by:

- preserving the authority of each GoreeCloud Integral Platform System;
- refusing to convert UI state into stronger operational claims than the underlying evidence supports;
- preferring bounded read-only integrations before write-capable administration;
- keeping private content, credentials, and raw sensitive diagnostics out of ordinary dashboards;
- treating current Glaze UI adoption, runtime acceptance, recovery, and production approval as evidence-gated work.

These are product objectives. Verified current functionality is documented in `FEATURES.md` and `CAPABILITIES.md`; future work remains in `FEATURE-ROADMAP.md`.
